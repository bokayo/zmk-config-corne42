Legend

  • Home-row mod — tap the letter, hold for a modifier
  • Layer key — tap sends a key, hold shifts layer
  • Held now — the key you hold to reach this layer
  • Arrows / edit — navigation & ⌘ clipboard
  • Transparent — falls through to the base layer
  • Dead — physical q / p are disabled on purpose
